## What today's models do and do not measure

Compact latency models use query identity, current measured file count and client-pending state. They do not use demand forecasts or future-load predictions. Complete60/300s offered history is currently an eligibility rule, not a forecast feature. These experiments therefore cannot demonstrate that forecasting improves maintenance timing. Full-history filtering removes the first five minutes of600s episodes; all compact-now early interference and any requests still queued at the horizon are outside this eligible latency evaluation. Preserve that population limitation. Never replace missing early history with zeros. Primary metrics so far are service latency, not total response time, SLA loss or whole-horizon system utility.

Pending-True eligible development support is22train/25validation queries, fewer independent episodes; rewrite support is4train/4validation operations. Many query/layout/pending combinations may be thin even if their marginal counts are nonzero. No uncertainty calibration, robust generalization or stable interference estimator follows from those counts. Twelve total rewrites on one source table/engine are inadequate for broad cost claims.
